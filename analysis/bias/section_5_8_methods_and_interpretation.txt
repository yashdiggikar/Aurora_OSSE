============================================================
Aurora Section 5.8 Spatial Bias Analysis — Methods and Interpretation
============================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Identify WHERE in the globe Aurora's systematic errors (biases) are concentrated,
and quantify where each insertion type corrects vs. worsens those biases.

BIAS DEFINITION
---------------
Bias(lat, lon) = mean_t [ Aurora(lat,lon,t) − ERA5(lat,lon,t) ]
Computed over all mature-phase steps (day 30–60, 60 × 12-hr fields).

VARIABLES ANALYSED
------------------
T500:    500-hPa temperature (K) — Aurora's thermodynamic state
U500:    500-hPa zonal wind (m/s) — jet stream representation
Z500:    500-hPa geopotential (m²/s²) — pressure pattern
Wind500: 500-hPa wind speed (m/s) — total kinetic energy proxy
(V500 computed internally but not shown as primary figure variable)

BIAS REDUCTION METRIC
---------------------
Bias reduction = |control_bias| - |insertion_bias|
  > 0: insertion reduces systematic error (improvement)
  < 0: insertion increases systematic error (failure mode)

This uses absolute bias to avoid sign cancellation: a run that shifts the
bias from +2 K to -2 K has zero "improvement" by this metric even though
it crossed zero — it just moved the error to the other side.

INTERPRETATION GUIDE
--------------------
- Large positive bias in T500 over polar regions: Aurora may be running too warm
  (possibly overcorrects toward climatology after long leads)
- Z500 positive bias over high-latitude continents: Aurora may be displacing the
  polar vortex or jet stream poleward
- Regions where ALL insertions worsen bias: Aurora's dynamics in those areas are
  inconsistent — forcing the state toward ERA5 truth in one variable
  creates compensating errors in others (balance inconsistency)
- Tropical regions where insertion helps: suggests Aurora's tropical dynamics
  are constrained by the inserted variable more than expected from theory
